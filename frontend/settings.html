Preferences

Currency, date display and the monthly income you are aiming for.

Every amount and chart updates with the chosen currency.

Used across the ledger, budgets, reports and goals.

Changes apply immediately after saving.

Appearance

Choose a light or dark workspace, or follow your operating system.

Theme The choice is remembered for every SmartSpend page on this device.

The theme toggle in the top bar also updates this preference instantly.

Notifications

Choose which alerts appear in the bell and on the budget and goal screens.

Budget alerts Warn when a category crosses 80% or goes over its monthly limit.
Goal reminders Surface goals that are close to their deadline and behind schedule.
Weekly digest A short summary of transactions, spending and net savings.
Recurring reminders Tell me when a scheduled recurring transaction is due to post.

Categories

Rename, recolour or add categories. Default categories cannot be deleted.

Data & backup

Download a portable copy, import a backup or start again with fresh sample data.

Your workspace is stored in the local SQLite database. Export a JSON backup before clearing app data, and never upload a backup containing real financial information.
